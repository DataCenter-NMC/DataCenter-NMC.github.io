About

Research that connects rigor with practical care.

Data Center, Northern Medical Center brings together clinical mentorship, quantitative methods, and thoughtful technology development. Our goal is to help turn fragmented information into evidence that people can inspect, understand, and use.

We emphasize transparent assumptions, reproducible workflows, uncertainty-aware analysis, and implementation in communities and settings that are often underrepresented in health technology.

Featured work

Diagnostic Odyssey Ledger

Prototype in development

Making the hidden burden of delayed recognition in invisible illness easier to see, examine, and act on.

Diagnostic Odyssey Ledger is an AI-assisted, evidence-linked prototype exploring how fragmented health and public data can support transparent estimates of the time, cost, and access burdens associated with prolonged diagnostic journeys.

Designed around

  • 01 U.S. open data and traceable evidence
  • 02 Human-readable assumptions and uncertainty
  • 03 Equity-aware views for local decision-making
  • 04 Human oversight for AI-assisted workflows
Research principles

The standards behind the work.

01

Human-centered

Begin with the needs of patients, clinicians, communities, and public-health partners.

02

Transparent

Make sources, assumptions, limitations, and uncertainty visible rather than hiding them.

03

Responsible

Design AI for privacy, fairness, safety, appropriate use, and meaningful human oversight.

04

Reproducible

Use documented methods, open standards, and reusable outputs wherever possible.
